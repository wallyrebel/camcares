About the Founder

A Message from Cam

Welcome to the official page of The Cam Cares Foundation. Our organization is dedicated to uplifting communities, strengthening families, and investing in youth through education, economic empowerment, and compassionate service.

Guided by faith and grounded in love for our community, our mission is to create generational impact by advancing opportunity, restoring hope, and building a stronger future for Ripley and beyond.

"Together, we are building more than programs—we are building people, purpose, and possibility."

This work is only possible because of supporters like you. Whether through volunteering, partnering, or simply sharing our mission, your involvement helps us expand our reach and deepen our impact.

Thank you for believing in this vision and walking alongside us.

Education First

Investing in the next generation through scholarships, academic resources, and mentorship programs for high school seniors.

Economic Empowerment

Uplifting local small businesses and youth entrepreneurs with events like our Pop-Ups at the Town Square.

Compassionate Service

Providing seasonal outreach, support networks, and basic necessities to families and children facing instability.

Ripley High School Senior Scholarship

Supporting graduating seniors who demonstrate financial need, academic achievement, and active community leadership.

Eligibility Criteria:

  • Must be a graduating senior at Ripley High School.
  • Acceptance to an accredited college, university, or trade school for Fall 2026.
  • Demonstrated record of local community service or volunteering.
  • Maintained a GPA reflecting academic diligence.

Application Requirements:

  • Application Form: Completed student details.
  • Personal Essay: 500-800 words detailing educational goals, career ambitions, or life mission plans.
  • Resume / Log: Log of community service hours or school activities.
  • Supporting docs: Proof of college acceptance & official transcript.

To apply or receive the formal application forms, please contact us or the RHS guidance office.

Pop-Up at the Square

Bringing Ripley together to support small businesses, highlight youth entrepreneurs, and strengthen families.

Event Highlights:

  • Local Vendors: Showcasing arts, foods, clothing, and small crafts.
  • Youth Marketplace: Designated free booths for youth entrepreneurs under 18 to build business skills.
  • Family Activities: Fun games, music, and seasonal activities.
  • Outreach Focus: Information tables on local support, tutoring, and family resource kits.

We are always looking for local vendors, youth sellers, musical guests, and volunteers to help set up and clean up. Vendor spaces are low-cost, and youth spots are completely free of charge.
